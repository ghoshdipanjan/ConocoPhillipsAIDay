# Welcome to ConocoPhillips AI Day!

We're glad you're here! This hands-on workshop is your opportunity to explore how Microsoft Copilot can help you turn data into insights, collaborate across teams, and create clear, compelling communications.

Bring your curiosity, experiment with prompts, and learn together. No perfect prompts required!

## Start here: workshop exercises

**[Open the wiki for the full exercise list and step-by-step instructions](https://github.com/ghoshdipanjan/ConocoPhillipsAIDay/wiki).**

The wiki guides you through five sections:

1. **Before the Shutdown: Three People, One Evidence Trail** - start with the shared story and context.
2. **Asset Integrity Exercise** - explore the asset integrity scenario.
3. **Finance Simulation Exercise** - explore the finance scenario.
4. **Supply Chain Simulation Exercise** - explore the supply chain scenario.
5. **Cross-functional Review** - bring the work together.

**Recommended path:** complete section 1, choose section 2, 3, or 4, then finish with section 5. For more practice, work through all five sections.

Each scenario follows the same workflow: **Chat → Excel → Word → PowerPoint → Outlook**.

## Workshop materials

Download [ConocoPhillips_Copilot_Demo.xlsx](./ConocoPhillips_Copilot_Demo.xlsx) and follow the instructions for your chosen exercise. The workbook contains multiple worksheets for the different scenarios. Additional files are available in the [Synthetic data folder](./Synthetic%20data).

These exercises use synthetic data and a fictional training story. They do not represent real approvals or operational outcomes.

## Make the most of the day

- Try different prompts and compare the results with fellow attendees.
- Review Copilot's responses against the source data rather than taking them at face value.
- If something is unclear, ask Copilot to explain or ask a facilitator for help.
- Once you've completed the exercises, try the techniques with content you're authorized to use.
